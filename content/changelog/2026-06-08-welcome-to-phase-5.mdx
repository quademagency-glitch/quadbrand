# Welcome to Phase 5: Growth & Retention

We are incredibly excited to roll out the latest suite of features for QuadBrand:

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard
Now you can track your generation velocity! The new Analytics dashboard provides a 30-day breakdown of all images generated by your workspace, grouped by **Brand**, **Aspect Ratio**, and **Time**.

### 🎁 Refer & Earn
Spread the word about QuadBrand and get rewarded. For every friend you refer using your unique link, **both you and your friend will receive 50 free credits** upon their sign-up. 

### 💌 Email Triggers
QuadBrand now proactively keeps you in the loop. You'll receive automated emails welcoming you to the platform and alerting you when your credit balance drops below 20.

We can't wait to see what you build. Happy generating!
