# BrandForge v1.0 is Here!

Today marks a massive milestone for our team. We are launching **BrandForge v1.0** to the public! 🚀

After months of building, iterating, and testing with our early beta users, we are finally ready to open the doors. 

## What's New?

- **AI Image Generation**: Create stunning visual assets tailored strictly to your brand guidelines.
- **Multi-Variant Generation**: Want options? Generate up to 4 variants of a single prompt simultaneously.
- **Background Removal**: Strip backgrounds from generated images with a single click.
- **Vectorization**: Convert pixel-based logos and graphics into crisp, scalable SVGs instantly.
- **REST API**: Integrate BrandForge directly into your own tools using our new developer API.

## What's Next?

We are already hard at work on the next set of features. Over the coming weeks, you can expect:
1. Video Generation Support
2. Custom LoRA model training on your own existing assets
3. Advanced analytics and team collaboration workflows

Thank you for joining us on this journey! If you have any feedback, please drop us an email or use the chat widget in the dashboard.
